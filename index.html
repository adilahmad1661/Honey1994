About us

Three generations
of beekeepers

Golden Hive began in 1987 with six hives behind a family orchard. Today our apiary is home to more than two hundred colonies that roam flowering meadows far from roads and industry.

We never heat, blend or filter our honey. Every jar is traceable to the exact yard — and the exact week — it was harvested.
